VENDOR SERVICE AGREEMENT

Agreement between Acme Technologies and Example Vendor

1. SERVICES

The Vendor shall provide software development and maintenance services to the Company according to the requirements agreed between the parties.

2. LIABILITY

The Vendor shall be liable for all losses, damages and claims arising from the services without limitation .

3. PAYMENT

Payment shall be made within 7 days of receipt of invoice.

4. AUTOMATIC RENEWAL

This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one-year periods unless either party provides notice of non-renewal.

5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

All intellectual property created by the Vendor shall remain the exclusive property of the Vendor .

6. CONFIDENTIALITY

Each party agrees to keep confidential information private and shall not disclose such information to third parties.

7. DATA PROTECTION

The Vendor may process Company data as required to provide the services.

8. GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement shall be governed by applicable law.

9. TERMINATION

Either party may terminate this Agreement with seven days' notice.

10. AUDIT

The Company may request reasonable information from the Vendor regarding performance of the services.
